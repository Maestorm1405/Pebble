Pebble Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Summary: Pebble has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your clipboard, to-dos, files and notifications stay on your PC. Pebble contacts the internet only to get weather and song lyrics, and to measure your connection's ping during games.

What stays on your PC

  • Settings, to-do list, file shelf, teleprompter script, recent colors and launcher usage counts are saved in your Windows user folder (%LOCALAPPDATA%\Pebble). They never leave your computer.
  • Clipboard history is kept in memory only while Pebble is running. It is never written to disk or sent anywhere.
  • Notifications are read with your permission so they can be shown in the island. The recent list is kept in memory only and is cleared when Pebble closes.
  • Downloads tidying moves files only within your own Downloads folder, and only when you turn it on.
  • Error logs are written to %LOCALAPPDATA%\Pebble\error.log to help with troubleshooting. They are not uploaded.

What is sent over the internet

  • Weather: your approximate location (from Windows location services, if you allow it) or the city you type is sent to Open-Meteo to get the forecast. Pebble does not store your location.
  • Lyrics: the title, artist and length of the song you're playing are sent to LRCLIB to find synced lyrics.
  • Ping: during games, Pebble sends a standard network ping to Cloudflare's public address 1.1.1.1 to measure latency. No personal data is included.
  • Web search: if you choose "Search the web" in the launcher, your search opens in your default browser.

These services have their own privacy policies. Pebble sends them only what is described above.

Permissions

  • Location: optional, used only for local weather. You can deny it and type a city instead.
  • Notifications: optional, used only to show notifications in the island. You can turn it off in Pebble's Settings or in Windows Settings.

Children

Pebble does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes
